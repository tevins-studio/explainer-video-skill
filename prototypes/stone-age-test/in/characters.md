# Characters — fixed descriptions

Every character is drawn by one rig (`src/toon/Figure.tsx`) from one design record
(`src/toon/cast.tsx`). The text below and the record must say the same thing.

## The monkey presenter (recurring, every video)
Brown monkey. Light cream/tan face mask (two eye lobes and a muzzle, outlined as one shape),
dark brown nose. Round monkey ears, brown with a tan inner ear. A small dark-brown tuft of hair
on top of the head. Large expressive eyes with brows. Blue short-sleeved collared shirt with a
white collar and a red tie. Dark trousers. Cream hands and feet. A long brown curling tail.
Personality: friendly, cheeky, curious; he tries things and reacts honestly.

## Ama — gatherer
Medium-brown skin. Auburn hair with a long braid over the shoulder. Ochre hide dress with a
cream fur collar and a fringed hem, a teal-and-gold bead necklace, leather foot wraps.

## Tuk — child
Tan skin, spiky dark hair, small brown hide tunic with a fringed hem, bare feet.
About two thirds of an adult's height, head relatively larger.

## Oru — toolmaker
Deep-brown skin, curly black hair, full short beard, red-ochre headband. Brown fur vest with a
cream fur edge, bare arms, ochre leggings, leather belt with a pouch, leather foot wraps.

## Nana — elder
Light skin with wrinkles, grey hair in a top-knot. Long green-grey cloak with a fur collar,
long sleeves, leather wraps. Carries a staff.

## Bek — hunter
Light skin, chestnut hair in a short bob, moustache. Teal-dyed hide tunic with a fur collar
and short sleeves, brown leggings, belt.
