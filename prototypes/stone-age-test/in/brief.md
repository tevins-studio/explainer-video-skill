# Brief — Stone Age style test (approved direction, 2026-11-24)

- **Goal:** a 30–40 s, five-scene visual test of the new house style, before the 8–10 minute
  documentary. The full documentary waits until the user has watched and approved this test.
- **Reference:** Ink Explainer, "How Long Would You Survive in Every Era of Earth?"
  (youtu.be/aY9gD8_wQs4) for the level of quality and presentation only. Own artwork,
  characters and compositions; nothing copied. Only the thumbnail and three small frames could
  be studied: YouTube's bot check blocked the video itself.
- **Style:** full-colour cartoon, large heads, clear expressions, recognisable clothing, clean
  consistent outlines (one ink weight, warm near-black), one hard shadow tone per fill, layered
  environments (foreground, middle ground, distance) with aerial perspective. Polished, clear,
  not childish, not cluttered.
- **Presenter:** the channel's monkey (see characters.md) in every scene: interacting,
  demonstrating, pointing things out, reacting. Design preserved; only quality improved.
- **Scenes:** 1) presenter close-up, speaking to camera, then a time-travel transition;
  2) Ice Age landscape: glacier, river, mammoths, the monkey interacting; 3) gathering berries:
  woman and child, the monkey joins in and reacts; 4) making stone tools: close-up knapping,
  the monkey tries it; 5) campfire: group, expressive acting, cinematic ending.
- **Voice:** Piper (free, local). Not eSpeak for anything that ships.
- **Budget:** free tools only. No paid services or credits without asking first.
- **Approval gates:** design sheets and style frames → test video → full documentary.
