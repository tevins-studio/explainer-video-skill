# Brief — A day in the life of prehistoric humans (~11,000 years ago)

- **What:** 3-scene animated prototype for the YouTube channel. Each scene about 5–8 s.
- **Audience:** general YouTube viewers, family friendly.
- **Mood:** playful and warm, gentle bouncy motion.
- **Style (channel house style, follow closely):** simple hand-drawn stick figures, black
  hand-drawn outlines, round heads, simple limbs, minimal faces. Colour: mostly black, white and
  cream with small brown, tan, blue and red accents. No realism, 3D, textures or gradients.
- **Presenter:** the channel's recurring monkey guide, a stick figure with monkey ears, muzzle and
  tail, a small blue shirt and a red tie. Visible in every scene as a friendly tour guide.
- **Scenes:** 1) the prehistoric landscape, guide walks in; 2) everyday survival: gathering food,
  making stone tools, guide demonstrates; 3) around the campfire, guide explains the evening.
- **Tools:** free only, no paid APIs. All art drawn in code (SVG in Remotion), no image model.
- **Voice:** one speaker, the monkey guide. Preview voice: espeak-ng + MBROLA `mb-us2` (free,
  offline). A nicer voice can replace it for the final cut.
- **Subtitles:** burned in (prototype default; confirm for the final).
- **Brand:** none beyond the channel's visual style above. No logo, title card and closing slide
  only.
