# Characters (fixed descriptions; every scene draws them from the same code component)

## Monkey guide (`Monkey` in src/Stick.tsx)
Stick figure, black hand-drawn line. Round cream head with two round monkey ears (tan inner),
a tan oval muzzle on the lower face, two dot eyes, small smile. Small blue shirt (short
trapezoid over the chest) and a narrow red tie. Long curly tail from the hip, ending in a curl.
Same height in every scene: 1.0 × a standard adult figure.

## Prehistoric people (`Stick` in src/Stick.tsx)
Plain stick figures, black line, cream round heads, dot eyes. A few accents to tell them apart:
a tan hide tunic (short, zig-zag hem), brown hair tuft or bun. Children are 0.7 × height.
